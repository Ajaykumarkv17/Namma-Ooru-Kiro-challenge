# Product Steering — Namma Ooru

**Vision:** An AI-powered Tamil Nadu travel discovery and planning platform whose tagline is
*"Discover the Tamil Nadu you haven't seen."* It should feel like a modern travel product, not a
CRUD app or admin dashboard.

## Product principles
- **Discovery without friction.** Users can browse and explore destinations without ever touching
  AI. AI accelerates, it is never a gate.
- **Breadth beyond the famous.** Surface lesser-known districts and hidden gems, not only Chennai,
  Madurai, Coimbatore, Ooty.
- **Trust through grounding.** Facts come from researched, source-attributed data. Never fabricate
  hours, fees, coordinates, or history. Show "Information unavailable" instead.
- **Cultural identity + modern polish.** Tamil Nadu visual identity with premium, professional UX.
- **Demo-first.** Every hero flow (search → destination → chat → itinerary → edit → reviews →
  creative discovery) must be quick to show in 30s–3min.

## Hero experience
> "Plan a 3-day Madurai trip focused on temples, history and food."
> → understand → retrieve grounded info → day-by-day itinerary in logical order → useful details →
> conversational modification.

## UX principles
- Always provide loading, empty, error, and skeleton states.
- Clear CTAs; mobile-friendly; accessible by default.
- Distinguish AI-generated content (summaries, itineraries) from user/source content.
