# Skill: Tamil Nadu Travel Content

Write culturally accurate, grounded travel content for Namma Ooru.

## When to use
Authoring destination/city descriptions, tips, or category blurbs.

## Rules
1. Ground every factual claim in the researched dataset or cited sources. If a fact is not in the
   data, do not assert it — say "Information unavailable" or omit it.
2. Respect Tamil Nadu cultural and religious context; use correct place and deity names; avoid
   stereotypes. Prefer widely-accepted transliterations and note common alternate names.
3. Keep a warm, modern travel voice; concise and useful over flowery.
4. Separate stable info from dynamic info (hours, fees, festivals); direct readers to official
   sources for dynamic details.
5. Never present model-internal knowledge as verified fact for the chatbot — that content must
   come from the Knowledge Base.

## Output
Clear, grounded copy suitable for cards, city pages, and KB chunks, with any uncertain claims
flagged rather than stated.
